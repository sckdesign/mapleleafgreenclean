Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data security are extremely important to us.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Maple Leaf Green Clean. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and local privacy laws.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide via our quote, contact, and booking forms. This includes your Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Service Address. We also collect anonymized usage data via Google Analytics (if you consent to our Cookie Banner).

3. How We Use Your Data

Your personal data is used exclusively to provide you with cleaning quotes, schedule services, and communicate regarding your appointments. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses Google Analytics to understand traffic patterns. Tracking scripts are only loaded after you click "Accept" on our Cookie Banner. If you decline, no tracking cookies will be stored on your device.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact us directly at hello@mapleleafgreenclean.com.
